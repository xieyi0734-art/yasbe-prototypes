How it works

Built for continuous value flow

CYRO is designed to circulate — through multi-chain liquidity, deflationary mechanics, and smart routing that keeps value moving efficiently across the ecosystem.

🔗

Multi-chain by design

CYRO deploys natively on Ethereum, Solana, Base, and Tron — not bridged. Each chain hosts its own liquidity pool. When a transaction occurs, the system automatically selects an optimized route based on current network conditions.

Why it matters: designed to reduce settlement costs across multiple chains.

🧠

Smart routing

Multi-chain isn't just about being everywhere — it's about finding the right path. The CYRO routing engine evaluates gas fees, confirmation speed, and liquidity depth across all supported chains in real time, selecting a settlement channel per transaction.

Routing is algorithmic and does not guarantee optimal execution in all market conditions. Actual results may vary based on network congestion and liquidity availability.

Key mechanisms

🌐 Multi-chain

Multi-chain deployment

CYRO deploys natively on Ethereum, Solana, Base, and Tron. Each chain has its own liquidity pool. The routing engine evaluates gas fees and liquidity to determine the settlement path for each transaction — no bridging required.

🛡️ Compliance-oriented

Compliance-first supply

Designed with reference to applicable regulatory frameworks. Seed/Private/Public rounds require KYC. Team tokens are locked 12 months. Treasury is governance-controlled.

💱 Settlement

Stablecoin settlement

Payment requests are settled in USDC or USDT using CYRO as the routing asset. The protocol aggregates liquidity across chains to execute at the best available rate — no manual bridging, no delayed finality.

Frequently asked questions

What is CYRO?
CYRO is designed as a utility token within the CYRO PayFi ecosystem. It provides access to platform features and fee reductions for active participants. Regulatory characterization may vary by jurisdiction and applicable law. Availability of token functionality is subject to jurisdictional eligibility — services may not be available in all regions.
How is CYRO different from BTC, ETH, or meme coins?
BTC solves digital gold. ETH solves smart contract execution. Meme coins solve attention. CYRO is designed to support global payment infrastructure — multi-currency accounts, AI-assisted FX, stablecoin settlement, and a compliance-oriented framework.
Is CYRO a security?
CYRO has been designed with utility-focused functionality — to provide access to platform features, fee reductions, and participation mechanisms within the CYRO ecosystem. Regulatory characterization depends on jurisdiction and applicable law. Holders should consult their own legal counsel for guidance. Full terms are under the Platform Agreement.
Where can I buy CYRO?
CYRO launches on DEX at TGE with an indicative price of $0.03. Pre-sale is available at $0.02 during seed, private, and public rounds — subject to KYC and jurisdictional eligibility. Follow official channels for launch announcements.
What chains is CYRO on?
CYRO deploys natively on Ethereum, Solana, Base, and Tron. Each chain has its own CYRO liquidity pool. The routing engine automatically selects an optimized settlement path for each transaction — no bridging required.
Is CYRO compliant with regulations?
CYRO is registered with FINTRAC as a Money Services Business in Canada (where applicable), and intends to seek US MTL and European EMI authorizations. The token design is developed with reference to MiCA, FATF Travel Rule, Singapore PSA, and HKMA stablecoin frameworks. CYRO only offers services where legally permitted. This does not constitute a guarantee of regulatory approval — consult a qualified attorney for jurisdiction-specific advice.
Who is restricted from participating?
Residents of OFAC-sanctioned countries, FATF blacklisted jurisdictions, and any jurisdiction where digital asset participation is prohibited. Users must be 18+, not previously restricted from CYRO services, and pass standard KYC verification.
